# lab/out notice

`asm/` and `ctx/` are generated by `lab/split.py` from our own build of GBADoom (doomhack/GBADoom, GPL-2.0-or-later). The disassembly is derived from that GPL program and is distributed under the GPL, not this repo's MIT license. Original function names are removed; the answer key stays in gitignored `lab/secrets/`.

`viz_map.json` groups functions by their hidden source file. Only the Stage (C) and Creature (D) may read it; the crew (B) must never read it.

`target.gba` is our build of GBADoom (the private variant), GPL-2.0-or-later, with no WAD inside. GPL requires offering the corresponding source to anyone who receives the binary: Lane A publishes `variant.patch` (against doomhack/GBADoom@7763f5f) after judging, and on request before then. `link_info.json` holds the ROM section layout and compiler runtime helper addresses so `patch` works on any machine.
